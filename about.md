# About Pebble Methods Studio

Pebble Methods Studio is a collaborative platform designed to help our team share and maintain methods, workflows, and research insights.

## Our Mission

- **Documentation**: Make our methods accessible and well-documented
- **Collaboration**: Enable easy contribution and improvement of workflows
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Create a central hub for team knowledge
- **Consistency**: Standardize approaches across the team

## What You'll Find

### Methods (`/methods/`)
Detailed protocols and procedures for common tasks and experiments.

### Workflows (`/workflows/`)
Step-by-step guides for complex processes and pipelines.

### Blog Posts
Updates, announcements, and insights from team members.

## Getting Started

1. Browse the methods and workflows
2. Use the templates to document your own work
3. Share your knowledge with the team

## Contact

For questions or suggestions about the site, please reach out to the team.
