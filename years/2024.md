# 2024 Summer School Projects

Welcome to the 2024 Summer School collection! Here you'll find all the research projects, tutorials, and outputs from our 2024 program.

## 📊 Project Overview

The 2024 summer school focused on computational approaches to cell biology and mathematical modeling of biological systems. Students worked on diverse projects ranging from cell cycle modeling to biochemical network analysis.
