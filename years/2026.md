## Research Directions

### Complex Systems and Statistical Physics
*Faculty: Chao Tang, Lei-Han Tang, Zhen-Nan Zhou, Zhiyuan Li*

- Statistical physics description of living systems
- Non-equilibrium states and self-organization
- Network dynamics and data mining
- Multi-scale modeling methods

### Synthetic Biology
*Faculty: Zibo Chen, Fangzhou Xiao, Jin Fan*

- Genetic circuit design and construction
- Protein de novo design
- Microbial chassis development
- Biofilm formation mechanisms

### AI for Science
*Faculty: Tailin Wu, Zitong Wang*

- AI-assisted scientific discovery
- Machine learning in biology applications
- Data science integration
- Computational simulation and prediction

### Microbiome Engineering
*Faculty: Baiyi He, Zhiyuan Li*

- Microbial community interactions
- Quantitative analysis methods
- Bacterial ecology and dynamics
- Quorum sensing regulation

### Single-Cell Biology
*Faculty: Yihan Wan, Shouwen Wang*

- Single-cell multi-omics technologies
- Cell fate choice mechanisms
- Tissue development processes
- Lineage tracing techniques

### Protein Homeostasis
*Faculty: Rui Fang*

- Protein folding and degradation
- Ubiquitin-proteasome system
- Cellular quality control mechanisms
- Protein aggregate clearance

### Biological Aging Dynamics
*Faculty: Yifan Yang*

- Nonlinear dynamics in aging
- Statistical physics approaches
- Lifespan regulation mechanisms
- Disease modeling

### Developmental Biology
*Faculty: Hanqing Guo*

- Embryonic development research
- Morphogenesis physical mechanisms
- Optogenetics and imaging applications
