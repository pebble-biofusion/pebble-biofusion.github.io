# Welcome to Pebble Methods Studio!

We're excited to launch our new collaborative platform for sharing methods, workflows, and research insights.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Methods Documentation**: Detailed protocols and procedures for common tasks
- **Workflows**: Step-by-step guides for complex processes
- **Resources**: Tools, templates, and reference materials
- **Updates**: Latest news and improvements to our methods

## How to Contribute

Adding content is simple:

1. Create a new markdown file in the appropriate directory (`_methods/`, `_workflows/`, or `_posts/`)
2. Follow the template format
3. Submit a pull request or push directly to the main branch

## Getting Started

Check out our [Example Method]({{ site.baseurl }}/methods/example-method/) for a template you can use.

Welcome aboard!
