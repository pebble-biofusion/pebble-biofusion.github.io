# Cell Biology Projects

This collection features all summer school projects focusing on cell biology, including cell cycle modeling, regulatory networks, and cellular processes.

## 🧬 Featured Research

### [SpudCell Whole Cell Cycle ODE Analysis (2024)]({{ site.baseurl }}/summerschool/2024/spudcell-tutorials/)
Comprehensive modeling of the complete cell cycle using coupled ODEs.
- **Key Focus**: Cell cycle transitions and regulatory mechanisms
- **Methods**: ODE-based modeling, parameter sensitivity analysis
- **Outcomes**: Identified critical control points in cell division

## 🔬 Technical Approaches

### Mathematical Modeling
- **ODE Models**: Deterministic descriptions of cellular dynamics
- **Stochastic Models**: Randomness in biochemical reactions
- **Hybrid Approaches**: Combining deterministic and stochastic elements

### Computational Methods
- **Numerical Integration**: Solving complex differential equations
- **Bifurcation Analysis**: Understanding system behavior changes
- **Parameter Estimation**: Fitting models to experimental data

### Data Integration
- **Time Series Analysis**: Tracking cellular dynamics over time
- **Network Analysis**: Mapping regulatory interactions
- **Validation Studies**: Comparing models with experiments
