# Synthetic Biology Research Projects

Synthetic biology is an important direction of the Pebble Biofusion Summer School, combining engineering principles with biological knowledge to design and construct new biological systems.

## Research Focus

### Genetic Circuit Design and Construction
- **Synthetic Genetic Circuits**: Design gene expression regulation systems with specific functions
- **Logic Gate Implementation**: Implement logic gate-like functions in cells
- **Oscillators and Switches**: Build periodic expression and controllable gene systems

### Biological Chassis Development
- **Model Organism Engineering**: Optimize common experimental organisms as biological chassis
- **Stress Resistance Enhancement**: Improve organism tolerance to environmental stress
- **Metabolic Engineering**: Reconstruct metabolic pathways to produce target compounds

### Bacterial Biofilm Research
- **Biofilm Formation Mechanisms**: Molecular basis of bacterial group behavior
- **Anti-Biofilm Strategies**: Develop methods to control and treat biofilm-related infections
- **Quorum Sensing**: Synthetic biology engineering of bacterial communication systems

## Typical Research Questions

### Genetic Circuit Design
- How to design stable and reliable gene expression control systems?
- How to implement complex logic computing functions?
- How to improve robustness and predictability of genetic circuits?

### Protein Engineering
- How to design proteins with new functions from scratch?
- What is the quantitative relationship between protein structure and function?
- How to improve protein stability and activity?

### Microbiome Engineering
- How to regulate the structure and function of microbial communities?
- How to design synthetic microbial ecosystems?
- Applications of microbiome engineering in medicine and environment?

## Research Methods and Tools

### Molecular Biology Techniques
- **CRISPR/Cas9**: Gene editing and regulation
- **Golden Gate Assembly**: Efficient DNA fragment assembly
- **Gibson Assembly**: Seamless connection of large DNA fragments

### Computational Design Tools
- **CADD**: Protein design software
- **Rosetta**: Protein structure prediction and design
- **GeneDesigner**: Gene sequence design tools

### Experimental Validation Methods
- **Fluorescence Reporters**: Real-time monitoring of gene expression
- **Flow Cytometry**: Single-cell level analysis
- **High-Throughput Screening**: Rapid screening of large numbers of mutants
